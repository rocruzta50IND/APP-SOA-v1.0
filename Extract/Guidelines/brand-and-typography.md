# Brand Guidelines & Typography
(Extracted from .claude/skills/brand/references/ brand-guideline-template.md, typography-specifications.md, visual-identity.md)

## Brand Structure
- Quick Reference: Primary/Secondary colors, Font, Voice traits.
- Palette: Primary, Secondary, Neutral, and Accessibility ratios.
- Voice & Tone: Personality traits, Tone by context (Marketing, Support, etc.).

## Typography Specifications
- Base System: 16px (1rem), Scale ratio 1.25 (Major Third).
- Type Scale: H1 (49px), H2 (39px), H3 (31px), Body (16px), Small (14px).
- Weights: Regular (400), Medium (500), Semibold (600), Bold (700).
- Line Heights: Headings (1.1-1.3), Body (1.5-1.6).
- Line Length: 65-75 characters (optimal for readability).

## Visual Identity
- Logo Variants: Primary, Stacked, Icon, Monochrome.
- Clear Space: Height of the logo mark.
- Accessibility: Text contrast 4.5:1 (AA), Large text 3:1.
