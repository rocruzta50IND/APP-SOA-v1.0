# Copywriting Formulas
(Extracted from .claude/skills/slides/references/copywriting-formulas.md)

## Core Formulas
- **PAS (Problem-Agitate-Solution):** Problem → Agitate → Solution.
- **AIDA (Attention-Interest-Desire-Action):** Closing slides, CTAs.
- **FAB (Features-Advantages-Benefits):** Feature → Advantage → Benefit.
- **Cost of Inaction:** Urgency by showing what is lost without the solution.
- **BAB (Before-After-Bridge):** Transformation slides.

## Formula-to-Slide Mapping
| Slide Type | Formula | Emotion |
|------------|---------|---------|
| Title | Hook/AIDA | Curiosity |
| Problem | PAS | Frustration |
| Solution | FAB/BAB | Hope |
| CTA | AIDA | Urgency |

## Headline Patterns
- Contrast: "[Old way] is dead. Meet [new way]."
- Social Proof: "[Number]+ trust [product]."
